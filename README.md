我叫 [Albert Wenhui Tan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cELItK0AAAAJ&hl=en)，是人大高瓴人工智能学院的博士生，主要研究 LLMs、Embodied AI。

我相信开源是该领域（甚至是人类社会）发展的重要动力。因此，我想把我在读博/科研期间的经验，和一些个人看法开源，所以有了这个 repo：AI-PhD.SKILL

AI-PhD.SKILL 是一份面向 AI 博士生和研究者的开放笔记：从选题、读论文、做实验，到写作、交流、研究判断，以及 RL 和软件工程。既有个人经历与观察，也有可以跟着推导的技术内容。希望它能帮助你进入一个领域，把问题想清楚，把研究做扎实，也让别人更容易理解你的工作。

这里的 SKILL 指研究者可以学习和练习的能力。每篇文章都可以独立阅读，也可以沿着目录串起来读。

文中包含大量主观观点。

My name is Albert Wenhui Tan. I'm a PhD candidate at RUC-GSAI, and I'm studying LLMs and Embodied AI.

I believe that open source is a vital driving force for progress in this field—and, indeed, for human society as a whole. With this in mind, I have decided to open-source my experiences and personal insights gained during my doctoral studies and research career. This repository, **AI-PhD.SKILL**, is the result.

AI-PhD.SKILL is a collection of open notes designed for AI PhD students and researchers. It covers a wide range of topics—from selecting research problems, reading academic papers, and conducting experiments, to writing, communication, and developing research judgment—along with specific technical areas like Reinforcement Learning (RL) and software engineering. The content blends personal experience and observations with technical material that you can work through step-by-step. My hope is that these notes will help you navigate a new field, clarify your research questions, build a solid foundation for your work, and make your research more accessible to others.

The term "SKILL" here refers to the competencies that a researcher can learn and cultivate. Each article can be read independently, or you can follow the table of contents to explore them as a cohesive series.

Please note that the content reflects many subjective viewpoints.

推荐阅读：[Reward 决定 Policy](SKILLS/judgment/3-Reward-Policy.md)

## 文件组织 / Repository layout

```text
README.md
SKILLS/
  research/
  communication/
  judgment/
  reinforcement-learning/
  engineering/
assets/
  captions/
```

- 正式文章放在 `SKILLS/<topic>/`。保留连载编号，便于对应原稿；分类目录用于按主题阅读。
- 中文原文使用 `<number>-<title>.md`，英文对照使用同目录下的 `<number>-<title>-EN.md`。例如 `SKILLS/research/1-Research-Pipeline.md` 与 `SKILLS/research/1-Research-Pipeline-EN.md`。
- 每篇文章顶部提供目录入口与语言切换。新增文章时，在对应分类的表格里补一行。
- `drafts/` 保存未定稿内容，`assets/captions/` 保存社交平台配文；正式阅读目录只收录正文。

Published articles live in `SKILLS/<topic>/`. The original series numbers are retained. Chinese originals use `<number>-<title>.md`; English counterparts add `-EN` before `.md`. Add a row to the relevant table when adding an article, and include index and language links at the top of both versions. Drafts and social post captions live separately in `drafts/` and `assets/captions/`.
